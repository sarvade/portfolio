## Context

The analytics products I work on are used by 15M+ people across 19 countries. Everything they show starts as raw event logs.

## The goal

The company needs one version of each number, read the same way by teams in all 19 markets.

## What I did

- Define and manage the multi-step ETL that transforms raw event logs into the canonical datasets the company reports on.
- Shipped 18 new metric definitions with 12 months of rebuilt history.
- Own the documentation for those definitions, so teams read the same number the same way.

## Result

18 new metrics in production with a full year of history behind them, backed by documentation that keeps teams in 19 markets reading the same number the same way.
