## What was at stake

End-to-end latency for transaction data was two hours, so everything downstream was always working from data that was hours old, for more than three million transactions a day.

## What I did

I built asynchronous, event-driven ingestion on Kinesis, Lambda and SNS. Transactions land on a Kinesis stream as they happen, Lambda consumes them in small batches, and SNS notifies downstream consumers as data lands, so no stage waits on a schedule.
