## What was at stake

For a status-chasing frequent flyer, the miles balance isn't a feature. It is the product. The loyalty platform for elite travelers ran on Informatica batch jobs, so a traveler who landed couldn't see their miles until the next morning.

## What I built

I architected the platform on AWS, with Spark and Hive over Iceberg tables on S3. I built the ingestion with Kafka, Flume and ClickHouse, landing vendor API data at 99.9% accuracy against source, and converted the existing batch jobs to streaming for the daily flight volume.
