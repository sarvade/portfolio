## Context

50+ ETL workflows ran on Informatica, a licensed legacy platform.

## What I did

- Led the migration of the 50+ workflows onto AWS Glue (PySpark) and AWS Lambda.
- Put the migrated code under GitHub-based CI/CD.

## Result

Licensing costs dropped 65%, roughly $850K a year.
