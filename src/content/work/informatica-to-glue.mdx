## Context

More than 50 ETL workflows ran on Informatica, a licensed legacy platform, and the license was a large, recurring cost.

## What I did

I led the migration of those 50+ workflows onto AWS Glue (PySpark) and AWS Lambda, and put the migrated code under GitHub-based CI/CD.
