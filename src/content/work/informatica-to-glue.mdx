## What was at stake

More than 50 production ETL workflows ran on Informatica, a licensed legacy platform. The license was a large cost that recurred every year whether the workflows changed or not, and the logic lived inside a proprietary tool instead of in version control.

## What I did

I led the migration of those 50+ workflows onto AWS Glue (PySpark) and AWS Lambda, and put the migrated code under GitHub-based CI/CD. Every transformation moved out of a licensed GUI and into code that is versioned and deployed through CI/CD like the rest of the stack.
