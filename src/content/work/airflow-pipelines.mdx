## What was at stake

Data from vendor APIs and legacy systems had to land in S3 and Teradata, every run, correctly. Vendor APIs time out and legacy systems hiccup, so retries and backfills aren't edge cases. They are the normal week.

## What I did

- Designed and operated the production Airflow pipelines that integrate those sources.
- Made the DAGs idempotent, so rerunning a task replaces its output instead of duplicating it.
- Added reconciliation checks that confirm the data that landed matches the source.

## What I’d do differently now

- Put a schema contract at the vendor boundary, so an upstream change fails loudly at ingestion instead of surfacing later in reconciliation.
