## What was at stake

Customer data carries privacy obligations under GDPR and CCPA, and a data lake is where those obligations are easiest to lose track of: many datasets, many consumers, one storage layer.

## What I built

A serverless data lake on S3. I moved 15 TB into it with AWS Database Migration Service, cataloged it in the Glue Data Catalog so it is discoverable and queryable, and governed it with Lake Formation, applying the access controls GDPR and CCPA require.

<Callout label="The principle">
  Governance is a property of the platform, not a policy document. Putting the access controls in the catalog layer means every consumer that queries through the catalog inherits them.
</Callout>

## Result

15 TB landed in a serverless lake with a catalog people can find data in and privacy controls enforced by the platform itself.

## What I’d do differently now

- Attach privacy classifications as tags when data lands, so Lake Formation’s tag-based access rules follow the data automatically.
